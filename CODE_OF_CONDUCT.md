# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We ask that all contributors, maintainers, and community members:

- **Be respectful** — Treat others with kindness and empathy
- **Be inclusive** — Welcome people of all backgrounds and skill levels
- **Be constructive** — Offer feedback that helps, not hurts
- **Be safe** — Report harmful behavior and support those who experience it

## What We Don't Tolerate

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
- Hate speech, slurs, or dehumanizing language
- Non-consensual sharing of private information
- Deliberate disruption of discussions
- Anything that makes others feel unsafe or unwelcome

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All GitHub issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Community spaces (Discord, forums, etc. if created)
- In-person events or meetups
- Any interaction related to rrresponseq

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a violation:

1. **Report it** — Email [maintainer email] with details
2. **Include context** — What happened, when, where, who was involved
3. **Stay safe** — We take all reports seriously and will investigate privately

All reports are confidential. We won't share your identity without permission.

## Consequences

Violations may result in:
- Warning
- Temporary mute/restriction
- Permanent removal from the community

We believe in second chances, but we also believe in safety.

## Appreciation

This is a creative community. We celebrate:
- Weird experiments and brave ideas
- Helping newcomers
- Sharing knowledge
- Building together
- Having fun making music

Thank you for helping make rrresponseq a respectful and creative space. 🎵✨
